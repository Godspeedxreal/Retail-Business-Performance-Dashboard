# Business Insights and Recommendations

## Executive overview

The retail dataset covers January 2024 to December 2025. Overall sales are approximately ₦2.86 billion, with profit of approximately ₦718.93 million and a profit margin of 25.11%.

## Findings

### 1. Sales and profitability
- Total Sales: ₦2,862,662,121.72
- Total Profit: ₦718,932,329.72
- Profit Margin: 25.11%
- Average Order Value: ₦367,007.96

**Interpretation:** The business generated positive aggregate profit. Management should monitor margin alongside revenue so that sales growth does not come at the expense of profitability.

### 2. Target performance
- Total Sales Target: ₦8,250,211,000.00
- Target Achievement: 34.70%
- Sales vs Target Variance: -₦5,387,548,878.28
- No month in the reviewed period achieved 100% of its sales target.
- January 2024 had the highest monthly target achievement at 44.65%; May 2024 had the lowest at 26.51%.

**Recommendation:** Review the assumptions behind target setting and investigate the operational or commercial factors associated with the gap. Compare performance by region, category, and month before assigning causes.

### 3. Product categories
- Electronics generated the highest revenue.
- Beauty & Personal Care generated the lowest revenue.

**Recommendation:** Examine product-level margins, stock availability, pricing, and demand within each category. Avoid assuming that the lowest-revenue category is unprofitable without reviewing its margin and cost profile.

### 4. Regional performance
- South West recorded the highest sales.

**Recommendation:** Study the product mix, customer demand, and sales practices in the South West, then assess which approaches can be adapted in other regions.

### 5. Sales channels
- In-Store generated the highest sales.
- Marketplace generated the lowest sales.

**Recommendation:** Compare channel-level profitability, order value, returns, and operating costs. Use this analysis to identify practical opportunities to improve weaker channels.

### 6. Sales representatives
- Fatima Agu led sales revenue at approximately ₦132.09 million.
- Emeka Akinola led profit at approximately ₦43.63 million.

**Recommendation:** Review both revenue and profit when evaluating sales performance. Share effective practices while considering territory, customer mix, and opportunity differences.

### 7. Returns
- Returned Units: 710
- Return Rate: 3.79%
- Refund Amount: ₦115,776,026.96

**Recommendation:** Investigate the most frequent return reasons and compare return patterns by product, category, region, and channel. Prioritize root causes with the greatest financial and customer impact.

## Important interpretation note

These findings describe associations and observed results in the dataset. They do not establish why a result occurred. Further operational investigation is needed before making causal claims or major business decisions.
